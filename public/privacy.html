AETHER MUSIC BOT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Overview

Aether Music Bot is a Discord bot and dashboard used to provide music playback and server configuration features.

2. Information we process

When you sign in to the dashboard using Discord, Aether may receive basic Discord account information required to authenticate you and identify the Discord servers you can manage.

Server configuration such as playback preferences, channels, roles, embed preferences and other dashboard settings may also be stored so those settings can persist between sessions.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate dashboard users, determine which servers they can manage, provide bot functionality, save server configuration and maintain basic operational records.

4. Data sharing

Aether does not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers required to operate the bot, dashboard, database and authentication system.

5. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and preserve server settings.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the dashboard at any time. You can also remove Aether from a Discord server to stop the bot from operating there.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Aether project owner through the official Aether support/community channel.
